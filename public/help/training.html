Run the five-minute demo

A synthetic scenario for a separately seeded training environment. These sample users and records are not created in a production deployment.

Preparation

Use a local training instance started with SEED_DEMO=true and its own data directory. The setup README contains the training account credentials. Never seed the deployed production database.

Walkthrough

  1. Sign in as Ceylon Trust Bank. Open Asha Senanayake and inspect her four versions.
  2. Run the integrity check. Compare the address in versions 2 and 3.
  3. Sign out and sign in as Serendib Finance. Look up Sri Lankan NIC 199512345678 to detect a duplicate without seeing personal details.
  4. Sign in to the customer account. Approve Serendib's seeded request.
  5. Sign back in as Serendib, open the shared record and select Reuse verification.
  6. As Ceylon, create a new address version. Show that the older approved snapshot remains historical and cannot be reused as the current verification.
  7. Verify the updated version, request new consent, and approve the new version as the customer.
  8. Revoke consent in the customer account and show that Serendib loses access.

What to explain

The duplicate search reveals a match without disclosing the profile. Verification, access consent and reuse are separate decisions. Each change preserves history, and consent is scoped to a version rather than granting open-ended access to future information.

The walkthrough changes its training dataset. For another clean run, choose a new training data directory rather than deleting existing records.
