Institution workspace

Register and maintain identity records

  1. Use Deduplication to look for the customer's issuing country, document type and number.
  2. If a record exists, request customer consent for its latest verified version. A match does not grant access to personal details.
  3. If no record exists, select Register identity, complete the profile and attach supporting evidence.
  4. Supply the customer's portal email and record their permission to process the submission. If an account is new, securely give the customer the temporary credentials shown once.
  5. Review the submission, then select Verify record and record your review notes.
  6. Use New version for corrections or changes. Updates require a fresh verification before another institution can reuse them.

Working with shared records

Once a customer approves, the record appears in your registry. Select Reuse verification to record acceptance of the latest verified version. Consent for a historical version does not permit reuse of a newer version.

Inspecting changes

Select a version from the history to see its snapshot. Changed fields are highlighted against the preceding accessible version. Run check compares the accessible snapshots, links and transaction receipts with their ledger commitments.

Revoking an identity

The issuer can revoke an identity with a recorded reason. Revocation is final for that identity and prevents further updates and cross-institution reuse. Review the decision before submitting it.

Customer workspace

Control access to your information

Open Consent requests to review the requesting institution, purpose and requested version. Approve access for 7, 30 or 90 days, or reject the request. You can revoke an active permission at any time.

Sharing applies to one institution and one version. A new version requires a new approval. Revocation stops future access through KYC Chain; it cannot recall information that a recipient already downloaded.

If a request refers to an older version, ask the institution to request the latest verified version. Only your customer account can approve sharing of your records.

Consortium administration

Add institutions and review activity

Open Blockchain explorer and select Onboard institution. Enter its name, unique code and officer email. The system registers its signing address and creates an officer account. Give the displayed temporary password to the authorized officer through a secure channel.

The administrator can inspect consortium metadata and the audit trail. Administrator status does not grant access to customer profiles through the application. Export the visible audit feed as CSV when needed.

For a lost account password, contact the server operator. Recovery requires an operator-run command; there is no public reset link or automatic email delivery.

Account security

Sign in and manage your password

Use the credentials supplied by your administrator or institution. New accounts must replace their temporary password before opening the workspace. Use a unique passphrase of at least 14 characters.

To change your password later, open the account button in the sidebar and select Change password. Changing it signs out all previous sessions. Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or eight hours in total. Sign out when you finish using a shared device.

If an operation cannot complete

Refresh the record before retrying a stale-version error. If a transaction error persists, contact the administrator with the request ID. Do not submit the same action repeatedly while recovery is in progress.
